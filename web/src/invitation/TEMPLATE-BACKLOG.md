# Invitation template backlog

Twenty designs, built one at a time. Each brief sets the direction; the builder decides the details. Follow [TEMPLATE-GUIDE.md](TEMPLATE-GUIDE.md).

**Which brief is next:** the lowest-numbered brief whose `id` has no folder in `web/src/invitation/templates/` on `main` and no remote branch `template/<id>` (`git ls-remote --heads origin 'template/*'`). Build only that one. When all twenty exist, stop.

Every design must feel clearly different from the ones before it: a different opening moment, layout rhythm, art language and voice. Read the existing templates first so yours doesn't repeat them.

## Briefs

### 2. Marigold Mandap (`marigold-mandap`)
- **Mood:** joyful, daytime, North Indian, full of marigolds.
- **Opening:** two carved wooden mandap doors with brass knockers swing open inward, garlands swaying, to reveal the invitation.
- **Layout:** the page sits under a mandap canopy; functions hang as garland strands, each ending in a flower medallion with the function's details.
- **Palettes:** Saffron (marigold orange, turmeric, leaf green, cream); Rani (rani pink, saffron, gold).
- **Art:** marigold strings, mango-leaf torans, brass diyas, banana-leaf corners.
- **Signature touch:** tapping a garland shakes loose a few petals.

### 3. Royal Scroll (`royal-scroll`)
- **Mood:** regal, evening, Rajasthani court.
- **Opening:** a rolled scroll with a silk tassel and wax seal unrolls downwards to full height.
- **Layout:** the whole invitation reads as one long parchment scroll with painted borders; sections are separated by miniature-painting-style cartouches.
- **Palettes:** Maroon & Gold (dark); Indigo & Gold (dark); Ivory Parchment (light).
- **Art:** jali borders, elephants and peacocks drawn as flat ornamental silhouettes, gold leaf frames.
- **Signature touch:** the scroll's rolled edge stays at the bottom and unrolls further as the guest scrolls.

### 4. Temple & Kolam (`temple-kolam`)
- **Mood:** South Indian temple wedding, morning muhurtham, auspicious and calm.
- **Opening:** a kolam pattern draws itself line by line around a brass lamp; the lamp lights, then the page appears.
- **Layout:** a gopuram silhouette crowns the hero; functions sit on stone-step cards; banana-leaf and jasmine-string dividers.
- **Palettes:** Temple Red (vermilion, turmeric, banana green, ivory); Silk Gold (mustard, deep green, off-white).
- **Art:** kolam dot grids, brass bells that sway, mango leaves, jasmine strands.
- **Signature touch:** small temple bells chime visually (swing) as each section enters.

### 5. Ivory Editorial (`minimal-editorial`)
- **Mood:** modern, minimal, city couples; a magazine-style invitation.
- **Opening:** a heavy ivory card flips over in 3D to show the couple's names in large type.
- **Layout:** strong typographic hierarchy, generous white space, a thin rule grid, numbered sections, no ornaments; functions as a clean agenda table.
- **Palettes:** Ivory & Ink; Sage & Ink; Blush & Ink.
- **Art:** none beyond typography, rules and one monogram. Restraint is the design.
- **Signature touch:** names set in a large high-contrast serif with tight tracking; subtle scroll-linked letter reveals.

### 6. Haveli Jharokha (`haveli-jharokha`)
- **Mood:** heritage haveli, Jaipur pink-city warmth.
- **Opening:** carved jharokha window shutters open to a view of the invitation, with light spilling through the jali.
- **Layout:** each section framed in an arched window; blue-pottery tile bands between sections.
- **Palettes:** Terracotta & Indigo; Pink City (rose sandstone, ivory, teal).
- **Art:** jali lattices, arches, blue-pottery florals, pigeons on ledges.
- **Signature touch:** jali shadow patterns drift slowly across cards.

### 7. Celestial Night (`celestial-night`)
- **Mood:** evening and night ceremonies, romantic and quiet.
- **Opening:** a dark sky where stars connect, constellation-style, into the couple's initials, then fade into the page.
- **Layout:** deep-sky background with a slow-moving starfield; functions as points along a moonlit path; the countdown shown with moon phases.
- **Palettes:** Midnight & Silver; Dusk Violet & Gold.
- **Art:** stars, crescent moons, faint nebula gradients, fine silver line work.
- **Signature touch:** tapping the sky makes a shooting star.

### 8. Alpona (`bengal-alpona`)

- **Palettes:** Laal Paar (red and white with gold); Shola (ivory and gold with deep red accents).
- **Art:** alpona swirls, paisleys, fish and lotus motifs in white line work.
