# Sell the WhatsApp invite, not the AI clip

ShaadiPlannerPro should not become another AI video generator. It should turn the invitation templates it already has into **a 9:16 WhatsApp video invite**. Build it with deterministic template motion graphics: the couple's real photo, exact names, dates and venues, licensed music, and an end card that links to the per-guest RSVP page. AI belongs only as an optional, short "living photo" moment inside that template. Indian families already buy this product. WhatsApp is the main wedding channel for **57–79% of couples** depending on the survey, and self-serve video invites sell at about **₹999–1,499**. Custom or caricature versions sell at ₹1,500–5,000, and suites with an RSVP microsite at ₹4,500–10,000+. That top tier is the one ShaadiPlannerPro already half-owns. Cheap AI kiss and hug apps run on a different model. They apply fixed effect templates (a hidden LoRA or curated prompt) to one photo, produce 5–10 s clips that cost the vendor **$0.20–0.50**, and sell weekly subscriptions through heavy paid ads. That is a novelty and entertainment business with growing consent and regulatory risk, not a wedding utility. The recommended unit costs **₹1–3 for a template-only render** and **₹35–90 for a hybrid with one AI moment**. A fully generative 15–20 s reel costs ₹130–530. That makes **₹999 for a video invite pack** and **₹2,499 for a multi-event suite** comfortable on margin. The first step is a fake-door test and 10–20 hand-made, paid invites before any pipeline is built. Three of the owner's four hypotheses largely hold. The fourth, that "5 s is too short", is right for the wrong reason.

All INR figures below use an assumed rate of about ₹88 per US dollar. Most market price data comes from vendor and marketplace pages, which have a commercial interest. Etsy, Fiverr and Play Store pages could often not be verified directly.

## Indian families buy a correct WhatsApp invite, and the RSVP hub is the premium tier

Demand centres on a shareable e-invite, sent alongside paper rather than instead of it. In WedMeGood's 2021-22 survey, **about 91% of couples used e-invites**: 31.9% sent only e-invites and 59% sent both e-invites and cards ([WedMeGood](https://wedmegood.com/blog/wedmegood-indian-wedding-industry-report-2021-2022)). WeddingWire India's 2024-25 survey of 1,500+ newlyweds found that **58.6% used WhatsApp for all wedding communication**, while **54% still sent physical invitations** ([WeddingWire India](https://www.weddingwire.in/wedding-tips/annual-wedding-industry-report--c11025)). A 2025 survey puts WhatsApp use for sharing details at 79% ([IBEF](https://ibef.org/news/40-of-couples-spend-over-rs-7-5-lakh-us-8-750-on-wedding-venues-online-survey)). The dominant pattern is hybrid: 50–100 printed cards for elders, often carrying a QR code, and a WhatsApp image, PDF or video for everyone else ([vendor blog](https://premium-wedding-invites.pages.dev/blog/digital-vs-paper-wedding-invitations-comparison)). The scale is large. CAIT estimates **about 46 lakh weddings in the Nov–Dec 2025 window alone** ([Angel One/CAIT](https://www.angelone.in/news/market-updates/indias-wedding-season-to-driveover-rs-6-lakh-crore-trade-says-cait)). No source sizes the e-invite market itself.

Prices form clear tiers. IndiaMART lists WhatsApp animated invites at **₹299–1,499** ([IndiaMART](https://m.indiamart.com/impcat/wedding-invites.html)). WedMeGood's own e-invite store prices most video templates at **₹1,499**, with a range of ₹1,299–2,500 ([WedMeGood](https://www.wedmegood.com/blog/10-gorgeous-video-wedding-invites-that-cost-under-5k)). A caricature vendor charges **₹4,500** for a video invite ([WedMeGood Wedding Caricatures](https://www.wedmegood.com/profile/Wedding-Caricatures-2215248)). Premium Pune packages that bundle invite videos, per-event cards and an **RSVP microsite** cost ₹4,500–10,000+ ([Happiffie](https://www.happiffie.com/pune/premium-animated-wedding-video-invitations)). Printed cards cost ₹15–80 each, so a video invite that replaces even a hundred cards pays for itself ([FluxNote](https://fluxnote.io/guides/wedding-invitation-video-maker)). DIY invite apps have modest reach. Crafty Art's invitation maker sits at roughly 500K installs ([AppBrain](https://www.appbrain.com/dev/Crafty+Art/)). Done-for-you sellers capture the higher prices, and they compete on **24–48 hour turnaround, revisions "so you do not pay extra to get your names spelled right"**, community-specific themes (Sikh, South Indian, Bengali, Punjabi) and music ([Magical Star](https://magical-star.pages.dev/blog/video-invitation-vs-static-e-invite-india); [WedMeGood](https://www.wedmegood.com/blog/10-gorgeous-video-wedding-invites-that-cost-under-5k)).

The buyer splits in two, and that shapes the format. Parents send the formal invite, often in their own voice, to large WhatsApp lists. WeddingWire's message templates include versions written in a parent's voice next to versions signed by the couple ([WeddingWire](https://www.weddingwire.in/wedding-tips/how-to-make-wedding-invitation-video-for-whatsapp--c1120)). Vendors advise sending video to younger guests and static images to large lists and to relatives with low-storage phones, who struggle with 8–20 MB files ([Magical Star](https://magical-star.pages.dev/blog/video-invitation-vs-static-e-invite-india)). Couples, meanwhile, run Gen-Z weddings like social campaigns with **7–15 s reels across 4–9 pre-wedding events** ([ThePrint](https://theprint.in/opinion/the-dating-story/the-reel-story-of-gen-z-weddings-how-they-are-lavish-viral-and-broke/2463119/)). They also pay "wedding social media managers" ₹50,000–2.5 lakh for reels ([Mint via Magzter](https://www.magzter.com/en/stories/newspaper/Mint-New-Delhi/VIRAL-REELS-THE-TREND-MAKING-WEDDING-BIZ-A-WHOLE-LOT-BIGGER)). What spreads is humour, novelty and story. The "Rishtedar Saare Vahi Pakau" roast invite drew **221,300+ views** ([Business Today](https://www.businesstoday.in/latest/trends/story/rsvp-rishtedar-saare-vahi-pakau-hilarious-indian-wedding-invite-roasting-guests-goes-viral-457095-2024-12-12)), and Ghibli-style couple portraits became a trend in April 2025 ([Brides Today](https://www.bridestoday.in/wedding-ideas/story/studio-ghiblis-ai-generated-portrait-trend-transformed-our-brides-today-cover-stars-1192318-2025-04-01)). I found **no evidence of paid demand for thank-you videos or hashtag services**, and no Indian statistic on wedding-website adoption. The US figure is 82% ([BusinessWire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20240625948789/en/The-Knot-Worldwides-2024-Global-Wedding-Report-Reveals-Industry-Growth-Amid-Macroeconomic-Challenges)).

One design should therefore produce three outputs: a light 9:16 video, a still card with a QR code, and a printable PDF. Each should exist in parent-voice and couple-voice variants, with names that cannot come out wrong. ShaadiPlannerPro's per-guest RSVP links and schedule page already make up the "microsite" that vendors charge ₹4,500+ for. The missing piece is the video that carries guests to it.

## Kiss and hug apps sell one-photo effect templates through weekly subscriptions and paid ads

The mechanism is simple. Each "effect" is a fixed motion template: an effect-specific LoRA or fine-tune, or a curated prompt, on a base image-to-video model. The user's photo is used as the first frame, which is why identity holds at the start and drifts as motion grows. fal's Wan Effects says outright that "each effect applies a specific LoRA-based transformation", and it is built on open Remade-AI LoRAs ([fal](https://fal.ai/models/fal-ai/wan-effects); [Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/Remade-AI/Squish)). Kling's effects on fal require **exactly one image** and run 5 or 10 s, with presets such as hug_pro, kiss_pro and bouquet_drop ([fal Kling effects API](https://fal.ai/models/fal-ai/kling-video/v1.6/standard/effects/api)). Vidu offers about 200 templates, including **dreamy_wedding, couple_arrival, petal_scattered, love_story and live_photo**. Almost all take one image, in 16:9 or 9:16 ([fal Vidu](https://fal.ai/models/fal-ai/vidu/template-to-video/api)). Couple effects work best when one photo already contains both people.

The economics favour the app over the user. Vidu templates cost **$0.20 / $0.30 / $0.50** per generation (standard, premium, advanced) ([fal Vidu](https://fal.ai/models/fal-ai/vidu/template-to-video)). Kling effects cost **$0.056/s**, about $0.28 per 5 s ([fal](https://fal.ai/models/fal-ai/kling-video/v1.6/standard/effects)). The owner's Kling 2.5 Turbo Pro costs $0.07/s ([fal pricing](https://fal.ai/pricing)). An Indian wrapper, "AI Video Generator: kiss & Hug", charges **₹449/week, ₹699/month or ₹2,999/year**, with a two-person video costing 30 credits. Several such apps have passed 1M downloads, and some were pulled from Play in August 2026 ([ThePrint](https://theprint.in/tech/upload-anyones-pic-ai-will-make-intimate-video-of-you-kiss-hug-apps-test-indias-it-rules/3024195/)). Distribution comes from ads, not product pull. Forbes counted **2,500+ Meta ads for AI kissing apps and about 1,200 for hugging apps** ([Forbes](https://www.forbes.com/sites/rashishrivastava/2025/01/16/ai-kissing-apps-are-taking-deepfakes-mainstream/)), and one Indian advertiser had about 1,000 Google creatives ([ThePrint](https://theprint.in/tech/upload-anyones-pic-ai-will-make-intimate-video-of-you-kiss-hug-apps-test-indias-it-rules/3024195/)). The category leader shows that the model works at scale. PixVerse reports **100M+ users and $40M+ ARR** ([PixVerse](https://pixverse.ai/en/blog/pixverse-announces-series-b-international-expansion-ai-video)), and its effect trends drew 10B+ views ([KrASIA](https://www.kr-asia.com/aisphere-touts-pixverse-as-the-canva-for-video-generation-and-lands-the-funding-to-prove-it)). These figures are self-reported.

Two lessons transfer, and one warning. First, **a named template with a sample output beats a blank prompt box**. That is the most plausible explanation for makeadreels getting registrations but zero generations. Second, the photo has to be the first frame. The warning is that India's amended IT Rules (in force 20 Feb 2026) require synthetic video to be labelled. ThePrint found a kiss app with no label, no consent check on the partner's face, and uploads that stayed accessible after account deletion ([ThePrint](https://theprint.in/tech/upload-anyones-pic-ai-will-make-intimate-video-of-you-kiss-hug-apps-test-indias-it-rules/3024195/)). A wedding brand that sends videos to elders should not ship kiss effects. Hug, petals, couple-walk and "live photo" style motions on the couple's own joint photo are the defensible subset. They still need a consent checkbox and an AI label.

## Template motion wins on cost, correctness and Indic text, with AI as a garnish

Template rendering is about a hundred times cheaper than generation and fully deterministic. Remotion is **free for companies of up to 3 people**. Above that it costs $0.01 per render with a $100 monthly minimum ([Remotion pricing](https://www.remotion.dev/docs/license/pricing)). Lambda compute for a 1-minute video is about **$0.017** ([Remotion cost example](https://www.remotion.dev/docs/lambda/cost-example)). Hosted alternatives cost more per render: Shotstack about $0.05–0.10 per 30 s ([Shotstack](https://shotstack.io/pricing/)), Creatomate about $0.155 per 30 s 720p Short ([Creatomate](https://creatomate.com/pricing)), and Plainly or Bannerbear $0.69–2.94. They would also require rebuilding the four existing templates in each vendor's own format. Remotion compositions are React, so the existing JSX, CSS and SVG largely carry over. The CSS keyframes have to be rewritten as `useCurrentFrame()`/`interpolate`, and the free Remotion Player gives users a live preview at zero render cost.

The decisive technical reason is Indic script. FFmpeg drawtext has a documented history of broken Devanagari ([FFmpeg trac #9200](https://trac.ffmpeg.org/ticket/9200)) and needs a harfbuzz build to shape text correctly ([FFmpeg cvslog](https://lists.ffmpeg.org/pipermail/ffmpeg-cvslog/2023-June/137892.html)). A browser renderer shapes text through Chrome's own stack, so Hindi, Tamil and Telugu should render as they already do in the HTML invitations. That is an inference from how Chromium works, and it should be tested with `@remotion/google-fonts` before launch. The makeadreels ffmpeg assembly is therefore the wrong place to draw names.

"Premium" looks are reachable without generation. Ken Burns, 2.5D depth parallax from a single photo (Depth Anything V2 / DepthFlow ([GitHub](https://github.com/lloydjf/DepthFlow))), stock petal, light-leak and gold-foil overlay loops, and kinetic type all work this way. Background removal with BiRefNet lets the real couple be composited into a mandap or floral scene with **zero identity risk**, because every face pixel is real. A stylised caricature or Ghibli still via FLUX Kontext costs **$0.04** ([fal](https://fal.ai/models/fal-ai/flux-pro/kontext)). AI video earns one slot: a 3–5 s "living photo" moment dropped into the template as a video layer, with a parallax fallback if the user rejects it. Vidu's own blog concedes that AI clips are not reliable as a standalone 30 s piece ([Vidu](https://www.vidu.com/en/blog/wedding-invitation-video-ai)).

Music needs a decision now, because the app already accepts uploads. Instagram's library is licensed for personal, non-commercial use ([foximusic](https://www.foximusic.com/blog/instagram-reels-music-copyright-legal-guide/)). In Zee v. Nykaa a label sought ₹2 crore over Bollywood tracks in brand reels ([Intepat](https://www.intepat.com/blog/blog-zee-nykaa-music-copyright-instagram-india)), and courts look at the purpose of use, not the account type ([Gala Law](https://blog.galalaw.com/post/102mtty/zee-v-nykaa-india-what-every-brand-and-their-counsel-needs-to-know-about-musi)). Ship a curated royalty-free instrumental library (shehnai, sitar, nadaswaram moods) as the default. Hoopr clears Bollywood and regional tracks commercially ([exchange4media](https://www.exchange4media.com/media-others-news/rs-900-cr-in-lost-royalties-the-hidden-cost-of-unlicensed-reel-audio-in-branded-content-149424.html)), and AI music is 13¢/min ([Shotstack](https://shotstack.io/pricing/)). Also offer a silent export so couples can add a trending song inside Instagram on their personal account. ShaadiPlannerPro should never render a Bollywood track into a file it sells.

## The owner's hypotheses: three hold, one needs reframing

**Hypothesis 1, that generic "ad generator" positioning only fits niche use, is supported.** No source gives a post-mortem of a failed AI invite tool. Everything that converts is specific, though. PixVerse sells named effects, not prompts. Indian invite vendors win on community themes, correct spelling and turnaround. Generic tools such as VEED and Filmora already market "Indian wedding invitation video makers" without owning the category ([VEED](https://www.veed.io/create/invitation-video-maker/indian-wedding-invitation-video-maker)). "Wedding invite for WhatsApp, with RSVP" names a job with a deadline. "Ad generator" does not.

**Hypothesis 2, that five disjoint photos did not blend without a story, is supported, with a nuance.** Effect templates almost all take one image, identity drift compounds across separate generations, and Vidu concedes long AI pieces are unstable. Multiple photos are not the problem in themselves. Multiple *independent AI generations* are. In template motion, a 2–4 photo "story" montage (how we met, engagement, families) is a standard 30 s invite structure, because the template supplies the continuity that the AI clips lacked.

**Hypothesis 3, that one photo, a fixed template, 15–20 s, high quality and real images would work better, is largely supported. Refine it to "one required hero photo, optional extras".** One photo matches how every effect app works. Real pixels remove identity and consent risk, and a fixed template solves the blank-canvas problem. On length, Shorts of **11–20 s perform best** ([Quso.ai](https://quso.ai/research/how-long-are-instagram-reels)), and average Reel watch time is only about **8.5 s** ([Metricool](https://metricool.com/instagram-reels-length/)). Public Reels peak at 45–60 s for median views ([Socialinsider](https://socialinsider.io/blog/instagram-reels-length/)). But an invite goes to people who already know the couple, so completion and clarity matter more than reach. Default to a **15 s save-the-date and a 30 s invite**, with an optional 45–60 s story cut for couples who post publicly. One product detail matters here. The current couple photo is a JPEG of up to 160 KB, which is likely too soft for a 1080×1920 frame with a Ken Burns zoom. Video needs a separate high-resolution upload, and that is an inference worth testing.

**Hypothesis 4, that a 5 s clip is too short to sell, is right for invites and wrong in general.** PixVerse and the kiss apps sell 5–10 s clips by the million, so length alone does not kill a sale. The makeadreels clip failed because it did no job. A wedding video has to carry names, a date, the events and an RSVP call to action, and 5 s cannot hold that. Reframed: sell the *invite*, and let a 5 s AI moment sit inside it as one upgrade.

On format, export **9:16 at 1080×1920, H.264, 30 s or less**. Keep text inside the centre 1080×1350 safe area, with about 180 px clear at the top and about 400 px at the bottom ([HopperHQ](https://www.hopperhq.com/blog/youtube-shorts-dimensions/?amp=1)). Target 16 MB or less, and make a lighter version for elders' phones. WhatsApp Status now reportedly accepts up to 60–90 s ([Green-API](https://green-api.com/en/blog/2025/whatsapp-increases-status-length-to-90-seconds/)), so 30 s fits every client, old and new. The file-size and compression behaviour of WhatsApp Status is undocumented and needs a test on a real device.

At ₹999 even the worst-case hybrid cost (₹90) leaves a gross margin above 90% before GST and gateway fees. Price can therefore follow willingness to pay rather than cost. If conversion is weak, test ₹699 before discounting the suite.

Validation should cost days, not a pipeline. First, add a **fake-door "Download as WhatsApp video — ₹999" button** in the studio, beside publish, and measure the click rate among couples who reach publish. Second, **concierge-sell 10–20 invites**: hand-build the videos from the existing templates (screen-captured or a quick Remotion prototype), charge through the Razorpay Shop, and deliver within 24 hours. Payment is the only signal that counts. Third, run **an A/B preference test** of template-only against the hybrid AI moment, shown to couples *and their parents*. Ask which one they would forward to elders. Fourth, spend about $5 generating Vidu and Kling templates on 20 real Indian couple photos, and score identity, attire and jewellery fidelity. Fifth, **test WhatsApp on real devices**: Status, 1:1 and group sends on a low-end Android, to learn the real compression and size limits. Sixth, test Indic fonts in Remotion (Devanagari, Tamil, Telugu). Seventh, ship the couple-page footer badge with UTM tags straight away. Typeform reported that most of its traffic came from its "powered by" button ([TNW](https://thenextweb.com/news/why-typeform-is-awesome)), and every wedding sends hundreds of guests, some soon to marry, to that page.

The phased roadmap follows from this. **Phase 0 (weeks 1–2)** runs the validations and the badge. **Phase 1 (weeks 3–6)** builds the Remotion pipeline on Railway or Lambda, a high-resolution hero photo upload, the 15 s and 30 s cuts with a royalty-free music library, the ₹999 pack in the Razorpay Shop, and the couple page with its QR. **Phase 2 (season prep, before the Jan–Mar and Apr–Jun muhurat windows)** adds the per-event suite, parent-voice and regional variants, and wa.me reminders. **Phase 3** adds the AI moment add-on, with the consent and labelling flow, reusing makeadreels' fal and pg-boss queue for that single clip. **Phase 4** extends the weekly Claude template agent so that each new invitation PR also ships its Remotion composition. The template library then grows in both static and video form at no marginal cost.

## Conclusion

The makeadreels result was not really a verdict on AI video. It was a verdict on selling a capability instead of a deliverable. The successful AI-effect apps and the successful Indian invite vendors share one trait: the user picks a named, finished outcome and supplies one input. For a wedding, the outcome that families pay for and forward is an invite that is correct, culturally specific and WhatsApp-light, and that leads to an RSVP. ShaadiPlannerPro already owns the hardest part of that, the per-guest RSVP and schedule hub, and the cheapest way to make video for it is the React it already writes. AI earns its place as a ₹499 garnish, not the meal.

The biggest unknowns are behavioural, not technical. Nobody has measured whether Indian parents prefer a real-photo template or an AI-animated one, or whether families welcome automated reminders. Both questions can be answered with a fake-door button and twenty concierge orders before the first render server exists. Seasonality is the hard constraint. Demand peaks in Nov–Dec and the muhurat windows, so Phase 1 needs to be live before the next window opens, not perfected after it.
