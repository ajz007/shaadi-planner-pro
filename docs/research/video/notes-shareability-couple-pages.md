# Shareability, Distribution, Couple Pages and Reminders for Indian Wedding Content

## 1. Video length and format for WhatsApp Status, Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts

### Takeaway
WhatsApp Status no longer stops at 30 seconds. Reports put the limit at 60 seconds from 2024 and 90 seconds since a gradual 2025 rollout, though WhatsApp has not confirmed this officially. Reels and Shorts both accept 9:16 video at 1080x1920 for up to 3 minutes. Large studies find that Reels of 45–60 seconds get the most median views, but personal invites are usually watched by a known audience, so a 15–30 second cut with an immediate hook is the safer default. A cut of 30 seconds or less still fits every Status limit, old or new.

### Cited Findings
- WhatsApp's Status video limit went from 30 s to 1 min (2024). It was then reported going from 60 s to 90 s, rolled out gradually through beta in 2025. A video of up to 90 s now uploads whole, with no trimming — [Green-API blog, Jul 2025](https://green-api.com/en/blog/2025/whatsapp-increases-status-length-to-90-seconds/); [ProPakistani, Apr 2025](https://propakistani.pk/2025/04/15/whatsapps-video-statuses-get-an-upgrade/); [LatestLY](https://www.latestly.com/technology/whatsapp-new-feature-update-meta-owned-platform-testing-to-increase-current-video-status-limit-to-90-seconds-for-android-users-6784205.html). Caveat: in the April 2025 reports WhatsApp had not yet officially confirmed a wide rollout.
- YouTube Shorts can be up to 3 minutes long, uploaded through the app or YouTube Studio. The change took effect in October 2024 (one source gives 15 Oct 2024) — [YouTube Help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/15424877); [Piktochart](https://piktochart.com/blog/how-long-youtube-shorts/).
- Instagram Reels also allow up to 3 minutes, in 9:16 at 1080x1920 — [Kapwing 2025 guide](https://kapwing.com/resources/social-media-video-aspect-ratios-and-sizes-the-2025-guide).
- Socialinsider looked at about 974k Reels. Median views by length: 1–30 s = 4,700; 30–45 s = 8,564; 45–60 s = 10,374 (the peak); 60–90 s = 9,790; 90–120 s = 8,000; over 180 s = 4,428 — [Socialinsider](https://socialinsider.io/blog/instagram-reels-length/).
- Buffer studied 2.7M Reels and found that longer Reels had higher engagement among the people who watched them, though fewer people watched them through — [Buffer](https://buffer.com/library/instagram-reels-length).
- Metricool reports that average Reel watch time went from about 4 s (2025) to about 8.5 s (2026). The hook therefore has to land in the first 2–4 seconds — [Metricool](https://metricool.com/instagram-reels-length/).
- Quso.ai found that Shorts of 11–20 s perform best — [Quso.ai](https://quso.ai/research/how-long-are-instagram-reels).
- Safe zones for Shorts and Reels (third-party guides that disagree; no official Meta or YouTube spec was found):
  - Keep text out of the top 15% and bottom 15% of the frame — [Lilach Bullock](https://www.lilachbullock.com/youtube-shorts-aspect-ratio/)
  - Leave about 180 px at the top and 380–390 px at the bottom, which gives a usable centre area of roughly 1080x1350 or 900x1350 — [HopperHQ](https://www.hopperhq.com/blog/youtube-shorts-dimensions/?amp=1); [PostEverywhere](https://posteverywhere.ai/blog/youtube-shorts-aspect-ratio)
- Indian Fiverr sellers sell invite videos at lengths of 15–60 s; 60 s HD (1080p) is a common package — [Fiverr caricature seller](https://fiverr.com/grafly_studio/caricature-wedding-invite-video); [Fiverr save-the-date](https://fiverr.com/naznt56/create-wedding-invitation-video-or-save-the-date-invitation).

### Inferences
- Default export: 9:16 at 1080x1920, H.264, 30 s or less, with text kept inside the centre 1080x1350 area (about 180 px clear at the top, about 400 px clear at the bottom). One file then works on Status (including older 30 s clients), Reels and Shorts.
- Offer three lengths. These structures are a product recommendation, not sourced:
  - **15 s:** 0–2 s hook (couple's names or photo with motion and the opening music beat), 2–10 s the date and city reveal, 10–15 s an "RSVP / see details" end card with a link or QR code.
  - **20 s:** add 5 s of story or family blessing ("With the blessings of…").
  - **30 s:** hook, story montage, event list (Haldi, Mehendi, Sangeet, Wedding), date, then the CTA end card.
- Socialinsider's data favours 45–60 s for discovery-led reach on public accounts. Personal invites go to known contacts, so completion rate and clarity matter more than algorithmic reach. Offer an optional 45–60 s "story" cut for couples who post publicly.

### Gaps
- I found no official WhatsApp doc for Status file-size, bitrate or compression behaviour. The often-cited 16 MB media limit is not verified for 2025–2026 Status uploads; it needs a manual device test.
- I found no official Instagram safe-zone spec.

## 2. How Indian families distribute invites

### Takeaway
WhatsApp is the main channel for wedding communication in India: 58–79% of couples use it, depending on the survey. Paper still matters, though: 54% of couples sent physical cards in 2024, mainly for elders and close family. The dominant pattern is hybrid: a few printed cards for elders, then a WhatsApp image, PDF or video for everyone else, often linked by a QR code.

### Cited Findings
- WeddingWire India Newly Wed Survey 2024-25 (over 1,500 couples married in 2024): 58.6% used WhatsApp for all wedding-related communication, 54% sent physical invitations, and 18% announced their engagement on Instagram or Facebook. 77% hired photographers and 67% hired videographers — [WeddingWire India](https://www.weddingwire.in/wedding-tips/annual-wedding-industry-report--c11025).
- A 2025 survey found 79% of couples use platforms like WhatsApp to share wedding details — [IBEF](https://ibef.org/news/40-of-couples-spend-over-rs-7-5-lakh-us-8-750-on-wedding-venues-online-survey); [ShaadiNews](https://www.shaadinews.com/?p=44720).
- About 57% of Indians share engagement and wedding announcements on WhatsApp (WeddingWire India, 2023, about 25,000 respondents). This source is from 2023 — [Hello! India](https://in.hellomagazine.com/lifestyle/20230616302880/indian-wedding-trends/?viewas=amp).
- In WeddingWire's 2021 survey, 39% still preferred physical invites. This source is older — [WeddingWire 2021](https://www.weddingwire.in/wedding-tips/weddingwire-india-reveals-the-newlyweds-report-2021--c11025).
- "The paper invite is important for family elders." Planners have seen families go back to printed cards after trying digital-only invites. This source is from 2018 — [HT City via PressReader](https://www.pressreader.com/india/ht-city/20180506/281509341815329).
- The hybrid model is common: 50–100 premium printed cards for close family and elders, each with a QR code linking to a wedding website — [vendor blog](https://premium-wedding-invites.pages.dev/blog/digital-vs-paper-wedding-invitations-comparison).
- WhatsApp etiquette guides advise respectful wording for elders and consulting elders on the message text. Guests often receive a scanned card or a video invite — [WeddingWire India WhatsApp guide](https://www.weddingwire.in/wedding-tips/marriage-invitation-message-on-whatsapp--c3545); [WedMeGood WhatsApp invite guide](https://www.wedmegood.com/blog/how-to-create-a-whatsapp-wedding-invitation-a-know-it-all-guide/).

### Inferences
- The product should output three formats from one design: a 9:16 video, a still image (card plus QR) and a printable PDF. It should also offer a respectful elder-message template and multiple language versions of the forwarding text.
- Personal 1:1 sends carry more etiquette weight than broadcast or group posts in Indian families. Per-guest RSVP links, which shaadiplannerpro already has, fit this pattern.

### Gaps
- I found no reliable statistics on broadcast lists versus family groups versus 1:1 sends, on video versus PDF versus image shares, or on how often multiple language versions are made. No Reddit r/IndianWeddings data was surfaced by search.

## 3. Wedding websites and couple pages

### Takeaway
In the US, wedding websites are near-universal: 82% of couples had one in 2023. In India I found no comparable statistic. Indian sources position a website as the information hub for multi-day, multi-event weddings, reached from the printed card by a QR code. Shared photo albums with face recognition are an active Indian category.

### Cited Findings
- 82% of US couples had a wedding website in 2023, up from 70% in 2019; Canada went from 59% to 69% — The Knot Worldwide 2024 Global Wedding Report — [BusinessWire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20240625948789/en/The-Knot-Worldwides-2024-Global-Wedding-Report-Reveals-Industry-Growth-Amid-Macroeconomic-Challenges).
- The Knot 2025 Real Weddings Study (about 17k couples) reported an average of 116 guests. 72% of couples said making guests feel taken care of was their #1 priority — [BusinessWire, Feb 2025](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20250227544757/en/Weddings-Shine-Bright-in-2025-The-Knot-Study-Reveals-Couples-Prioritize-Celebration-Joy-and-Connection-Amid-Socioeconomic-Instability).
- Joy's core website, registry and unlimited-guest features are free. Joy's guest app includes a shared photo gallery for guest uploads — [Joy pricing](https://withjoy.com/pricing/); [Joy guest messaging](https://withjoy.com/guest-messaging/).
- Joy's origin story is that guests at its first wedding site asked for sites of their own. Joy credits "explosive organic growth" for its $20M raise in 2021. Both claims are self-reported — [Joy Our Story](https://withjoy.com/our-story/); [Joy Newsroom](https://withjoy.com/newsroom/).
- Indian sources say a printed card cannot hold 4–5 ceremonies over 3 days, while a website can give each event its own timing, venue and attire section. The hybrid model pairs a printed keepsake with a QR code as the "information hub" — [vendor blog 2026 trends](https://premium-wedding-invites.pages.dev/blog/top-10-wedding-invitation-trends-2026).
- Typical QR destinations are an RSVP form, venue directions, a gift registry and UPI gifting. Paytm describes an Indian couple who used a QR code for cash gifts in 2021 — [Paytm blog](https://paytm.com/blog/payments/qr-code/qr-codes-wedding-invitations-rsvp-directions); [Chococraft](https://www.chococraft.in/blogs/wedding-gifts/qr-wedding-invitations-gurgaon).
- QR codes "make their way down wedding aisles" (Aug 2026, international) — [The Star](https://www.thestar.com.my/tech/tech-news/2026/08/02/qr-codes-make-their-way-down-wedding-aisles).
- Kwikpic (Kolkata) is a face-recognition photo-sharing service. Guests scan a QR code or open a link, take a selfie, and get only the photos they appear in. Self-reported figures: over 400k events, over 3M users, 99.9% accuracy. These are unverified, and the press coverage is advertorial — [Kwikpic weddings](https://www.kwikpic.in/solutions/kwikpic-for-weddings); [Tribune advertorial](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/business/kwikpics-ai-technology-revolutionizes-event-and-wedding-photo-sharing).
- KnotPix is an Indian rival that collects photos through QR codes and links — [The Wire/PTI](https://m.thewire.in/article/ptiprnews/indias-weddings-go-digital-how-knotpix-is-helping-couples-collect-every-memory).

### Inferences
- shaadiplannerpro already has an invite, per-guest RSVP and a schedule page. Combining them into one couple page is a small step and matches the "information hub" positioning. The page would hold the invite video, the events with maps and dress code, the RSVP, a livestream link and a photo album, with a QR code on the printed card pointing to it.
- For photo upload, integrating with or linking to Google Photos or Kwikpic-style albums may be cheaper than building face recognition.

### Gaps
- I found no feature-usage data for India on which website sections guests actually open, and no US data either (The Knot and Zola do not publish it in the sources found).
- I found no usage numbers for WedMeGood, ShaadiSaga or The Wed Site couple websites.
- I found no data on QR scan rates from wedding cards.
- I found no data on livestream usage for Indian weddings.

## 4. Reminder and countdown messaging (WhatsApp Business API costs)

### Takeaway
Since 1 July 2025, Meta charges per delivered message, and India has been billed in INR since January 2026. Indicative India rates are about ₹0.86 per marketing message and about ₹0.115–0.145 per utility message, plus BSP fees and 18% GST. Utility messages sent inside an open service window were free from July 2025, but Meta's docs say they are charged again from 1 Oct 2026. For a 300-guest wedding, three utility reminders cost about ₹100–150 in Meta fees.

### Cited Findings
- From 1 July 2025, pricing moved from per-conversation to per-message. Utility templates sent inside an open customer-service window became free on 1 July 2025 and are charged again from 1 Oct 2026. The India marketing rate rose on 1 Jan 2026. INR billing began 1 Jan 2026, and accounts must move to INR by 31 Dec 2026 — [Meta pricing docs](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/pricing.md). The page does not show the actual rupee figures; they are in linked CSV and PDF rate cards.
- India marketing rate of about ₹0.8631 per message from 1 Jan 2026, up about 10% from about ₹0.7846. Utility rate of about ₹0.115 — [AiSensy](https://m.aisensy.com/blog/whatsapp-api-new-pricing/); [TelecomTalk](https://telecomtalk.info/whatsappbusiness-price-reveal-telegram-arattai-may-benefit/1012403/); [MyOperator](https://myoperator.com/blog/whatsapp-business-api-pricing-india-2026).
- Conflicting figures: one vendor lists marketing at ₹1.09 and utility at ₹0.145 — [Monty Mobile](https://montymobile.com/blogs/whatsapp-business-api-pricing-in-india-inr-rates-gst-and-the-2026-currency-migration-deadline). Chatlivo gives approximate ranges as of July 2026: marketing ₹0.88–1.10, utility ₹0.15–0.25, authentication ₹0.15–0.20 — [Chatlivo/DEV](https://dev.to/chatlivo/whatsapp-business-api-pricing-2026-india-rates-free-tier-21ed).
- Total cost is Meta's charge plus the BSP platform fee plus 18% GST. Utility messages get volume tiers from July 2025 — [MyOperator](https://myoperator.com/blog/whatsapp-business-api-pricing-india-2026); [WATI](https://www.wati.io/blog/whatsapp-api-pricing-guide).

### Inferences
- Event reminders ("Sangeet tomorrow 7 pm, venue map") should be approved as utility templates, which cost about 7 times less than marketing. Countdown "hype" messages risk being classed as marketing.
- A cheaper alternative is to have families send reminders themselves through click-to-send wa.me links or share sheets: no API cost, and the message reads as personal.

### Gaps
- I found no survey on whether Indian families want automated reminders, or whether they find them impersonal. Validate with users.
- I could not retrieve the exact official India rate card (the CSV and PDF were not fetched).

## 5. Viral wedding content trends, 2024–2026

### Takeaway
Viral content is well documented for humour or novelty invites (roast cards, Aadhaar-style cards) and for Ghibli-style portraits (April 2025). Paid reel production is now a business: Indian "wedding social media managers" charge ₹50k–2.5 lakh. I found little hard engagement data for AI save-the-date reels, caricature videos, AI baraat entries or "how we met" reels.

### Cited Findings
- The Studio Ghibli AI-portrait trend was called "the latest viral sensation." Brides Today turned its cover couples into Ghibli art in April 2025 — [Brides Today](https://www.bridestoday.in/wedding-ideas/story/studio-ghiblis-ai-generated-portrait-trend-transformed-our-brides-today-cover-stars-1192318-2025-04-01).
- Digit published ChatGPT prompts for wedding invites, including a Ghibli-inspired style — [Digit](https://www.digit.in/news/general/best-wedding-invitation-card-prompts-for-chatgpt.html).
- Mint (28 Feb 2025): "wedding social media managers" are a new service category charging ₹50,000–2.5 lakh for reels "that can rake up millions of views." I saw only the Magzter teaser — [Mint via Magzter](https://www.magzter.com/en/stories/newspaper/Mint-New-Delhi/VIRAL-REELS-THE-TREND-MAKING-WEDDING-BIZ-A-WHOLE-LOT-BIGGER).
- The "RSVP: Rishtedar Saare Vahi Pakau" roast invite went viral in December 2024 with over 221,300 views — [Business Today](https://www.businesstoday.in/latest/trends/story/rsvp-rishtedar-saare-vahi-pakau-hilarious-indian-wedding-invite-roasting-guests-goes-viral-457095-2024-12-12).
- An invite styled like an Aadhaar card went viral in December 2024 — [Business Today](https://www.businesstoday.in/latest/trends/story/wedding-invitation-styled-like-aadhaar-card-leaves-guests-amused-goes-viral-457309-2024-12-13).
- The Ambani–Merchant invite video (silver temple) went viral in 2024 — [The Quint](https://thequint.com/entertainment/celebrities/anant-ambani-radhika-merchants-regal-wedding-invite-video-goes-viral).
- Caricature invite videos and AI save-the-date reels are widely sold on Fiverr by Indian sellers. This shows demand but not virality — [Fiverr caricature](https://fiverr.com/mehak_jain/design-indian-wedding-invitation-video); [Fiverr AI reel](https://fiverr.com/jiya_singhal_/create-an-ai-animated-wedding-announcement-reel).
- AI video clips are not reliable as a standalone 30 s piece; stability varies with style and duration — [Vidu](https://www.vidu.com/en/blog/wedding-invitation-video-ai).

### Inferences
- Humour, novelty and personal story spread further than generic templates. Offer "story" and "fun" template slots, such as a how-we-met caption sequence or a playful family roll-call, alongside traditional designs.
- AI-styled portraits (Ghibli or caricature) work best as stills inside a template-driven video, not as a fully AI-generated 30 s clip.

### Gaps
- I found no verified engagement numbers for AI baraat or groom-entry videos, proposal-story reels, "how we met" reels or couple-hashtag campaigns from 2024–2026.

## 6. Growth loops: "made with X" watermarks and links

### Takeaway
"Powered by" badges are a well-known loop: Hotmail, Typeform and Calendly are the usual examples. Joy grew through guests who wanted sites of their own. Hard causal evidence is thin. The best data point is Typeform saying most of its traffic came from its "powered by" button. Wedding invites suit this loop well, because each couple sends them to hundreds of guests, and some of those guests will plan weddings soon.

### Cited Findings
- Typeform said in 2017 that "most of our traffic was from clicks on our 'powered-by-Typeform' button" — [TNW, 2017 (older)](https://thenextweb.com/news/why-typeform-is-awesome).
- Hotmail reportedly reached about 1M users within 6 months of adding its email footer link in 1996, citing Penenberg's *Viral Loop*. The "PS: I love you" line was a pitch, not what shipped. This is secondary and old — [OpenScout Substack](https://openscout.substack.com/p/distribution-you-dont-pay-for).
- Calendly's invite-based loop: about 4M users and $30M ARR in under 10 years — [OpenView](https://openviewpartners.com/blog/saas-product-viral-loop/).
- Typeform, Kit, Webflow and Calendly do not let free plans remove the badge; removing it is a paid feature — [Founderpath](https://founderpath.com/blog/powered-by-virality); [Demand Curve](https://www.demandcurve.com/newsletters/growth-newsletter-245).
- Joy: guests of its first wedding site asked for their own, and the company credits organic growth (self-reported) — [Joy Our Story](https://withjoy.com/our-story/).

### Inferences
- Add a tasteful "Made with ShaadiPlannerPro" end-card or footer link on the free tier, and let couples remove it on paid plans. Every guest who opens the RSVP or schedule page is a high-intent viewer.
- Track the loop with UTM or referral codes on the footer link. Typeform's experience suggests attribution is otherwise hard.

### Gaps
- I found no controlled study isolating how much watermarks contribute to conversion.
- I found no Canva-specific "made with" conversion data.
- I found no Indian wedding-app referral data.
