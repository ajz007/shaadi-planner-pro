# Indian Wedding Digital Products Market (video focus), 2024–2026

Source caveat: most price and trend sources are vendor or marketplace pages, which have a commercial interest. Etsy, Fiverr and Play Store detail pages returned 403 or truncated content, so their review counts, gig prices and install counts could not be verified directly.

## Q1. Which product types sell, ranked by apparent demand

### Takeaway
WhatsApp-shareable e-invites, as static images and short MP4 videos, are the core product. Combined e-invite plus physical card use is the norm. Caricature/illustrated video invites and multi-event suites (one theme covering haldi, mehendi, sangeet and so on) are the main premium variants. Wedding websites with RSVP and QR codes are an emerging upsell. I found no demand evidence for thank-you videos or hashtag services.

### Cited Findings
- WedMeGood 2021-22 report (older): 31.9% of users sent only e-invites, 59.05% sent both e-invites and traditional cards, and 9.05% sent only traditional cards. So about 91% used e-invites in some form. — [WedMeGood report 2021-22](https://wedmegood.com/blog/wedmegood-indian-wedding-industry-report-2021-2022)
- WedMeGood 2024-25 report (3,500+ couples, Apr 2024–Mar 2025): e-invites were the top "eco-friendly practice" at 49.2%. (This is framed as an eco-practice, not as total e-invite adoption.) — via search summary of [WedMeGood/industry coverage](https://in.eventfaqs.com/2025/03/25/whats-trending-in-indian-weddings-a-look-at-modern-preferences/) and [ShaadiNews](https://www.shaadinews.com/?p=44720)
- WeddingWire India 2024-25 newlywed survey (1,500+ couples who married in 2024): 54% chose physical invitations, and 58.6% used WhatsApp for wedding communication. — [WeddingWire India annual report](https://www.weddingwire.in/wedding-tips/annual-wedding-industry-report--c11025). A related WeddingWire article cites 57% using WhatsApp to announce the wedding. — [WeddingWire WhatsApp video guide, updated 26 Mar 2025](https://www.weddingwire.in/wedding-tips/how-to-make-wedding-invitation-video-for-whatsapp--c1120). The 57% and 58.6% figures conflict slightly, probably because they come from different questions.
- Vendor claim: the WhatsApp video invite is "practically standard" in Indian weddings. This is marketing language. — [Magical Star blog](https://magical-star.pages.dev/blog/video-invitation-vs-static-e-invite-india)
- WedMeGood's own e-invite store sells save-the-date templates and themes with "multiple layers" so one design covers several events, with ritual and community variants: Anand Karaj (Sikh), Vivaham (South Indian), Bibaha (Bengali), Amrit Vela (Punjabi), and a Beach theme for destination weddings. — [WedMeGood blog, 16 Mar 2023 (older)](https://www.wedmegood.com/blog/10-gorgeous-video-wedding-invites-that-cost-under-5k)

### Inferences
- Apparent demand ranking: (1) WhatsApp video and static e-invite for the main wedding; (2) a multi-event suite (haldi, mehendi, sangeet, reception), sold as one theme; (3) save-the-date video; (4) caricature or illustrated-couple video, a premium variant; (5) wedding website with RSVP and QR, strongest for NRI and destination weddings; (6) countdown and reminder messages; (7) thank-you videos and hashtag services, for which I found no evidence of paid demand.
- ShaadiPlannerPro already has RSVP links and guest pages, which is the tier vendors charge most for (₹4,500–10,000+). An MP4 video invite that links to the RSVP page is the natural bundle.

### Gaps

- No data on demand for thank-you videos, countdown messages or hashtag services.
- No volume data by ritual (haldi, mehendi, sangeet).

## Q2. Price points (INR) and volume signals

### Takeaway
Template video invites cluster at ₹299–₹2,500, done-for-you custom or caricature work at about ₹1,500–₹5,000, and premium suites with a microsite at ₹4,500–₹10,000+. Printed cards cost ₹15–80 each, so a video replacing 300 cards saves ₹4,500–24,000. App install bases for Indian invite makers are modest (hundreds of thousands, not crores).

### Cited Findings
- IndiaMART listings: WhatsApp animation wedding video invitation at ₹1,499 per piece, and an "E Invitation" video at ₹299 per piece. — [IndiaMART wedding invites](https://m.indiamart.com/impcat/wedding-invites.html)
- Magical Star: static e-invite ₹900–1,200, video invite ₹900–1,200 (the same price), revisions included, video delivered in 24–48 hours, static within 24 hours. — [Magical Star](https://magical-star.pages.dev/blog/video-invitation-vs-static-e-invite-india)
- WedMeGood e-invite store: most video templates cost ₹1,499. The range runs from ₹1,299 (caricature-style "Sky Shots", "Blooming in Love") to ₹1,999–2,500 (Mughal or premium florals). — [WedMeGood blog, 2023 (older)](https://www.wedmegood.com/blog/10-gorgeous-video-wedding-invites-that-cost-under-5k)
- Caricature vendor: Video Invite ₹4,500, with a 50-card minimum on printed cards. — [WedMeGood Wedding Caricatures](https://www.wedmegood.com/profile/Wedding-Caricatures-2215248)
- Premium packages cost ₹4,500 to ₹10,000+ and include invite videos, per-event cards and an RSVP microsite. — [Happiffie Pune](https://www.happiffie.com/pune/premium-animated-wedding-video-invitations)
- Outlier: VenueLook says e-invites cost ₹20,000–50,000. This is likely bespoke cinematic work, and it conflicts with every other source. — [VenueLook](https://www.venuelook.com/costs/invitation-prices-in-india)
- Printed invites cost ₹15–80 per card including design, print and courier. — [FluxNote](https://fluxnote.io/guides/wedding-invitation-video-maker)
- Etsy generic animated (Canva) templates: about $1.49–$7.99 (about ₹125–670), for example $3.99 on sale from $7.99. Nikkah/Walima animated invites: about $13–28 (about ₹1,100–2,350), for example $17.17. — [Etsy listing 1872876294](https://www.etsy.com/listing/1872876294/animated-wedding-invitation-video-custom), [Etsy listing 4607626528](https://www.etsy.com/listing/4607626528)
- Play Store search (India) for "wedding invitation video maker" returns many apps. The search page showed no install counts. — [Google Play search](https://play.google.com/store/search?q=wedding%20invitation%20video%20maker&c=apps&gl=in)
- Crafty Art (Surat, Gujarat) is the developer of "Invitation Maker - Card Design". AppBrain estimates 500K+ installs (100K–1M band) and says the developer has been active since 2022. ### Inferences
- The price anchor for a self-serve, personalised video invite in India is about ₹999–1,499. A caricature or real-photo custom version supports ₹2,500–5,000, and a full suite with a microsite supports ₹5,000–10,000.
- Low iOS rating counts and 500K-scale Android installs suggest DIY apps reach a fragmented, mostly low-paying audience. Done-for-you sellers on Instagram, WhatsApp and IndiaMART capture higher prices.

## Q3. Buyer and decision-maker, timing and channel

### Takeaway
WhatsApp is the dominant channel, used by about 57–59% of couples for announcements and communication. Parents and families send many invites, often written in their voice, while couples are the visible customers of e-invite vendors and drive reels and social content. I found no reliable data on lead times.

### Cited Findings
- 57% (WeddingWire article) and 58.6% (WeddingWire 2024-25 survey) of couples used WhatsApp to announce or communicate. — [WeddingWire guide](https://www.weddingwire.in/wedding-tips/how-to-make-wedding-invitation-video-for-whatsapp--c1120), [WeddingWire report](https://www.weddingwire.in/wedding-tips/annual-wedding-industry-report--c11025)
- WeddingWire's message templates include versions written in a parent's voice and versions signed "Bride & Groom". It notes that parents sometimes send invitations and that couples coordinate in WhatsApp groups. — [WeddingWire guide](https://www.weddingwire.in/wedding-tips/how-to-make-wedding-invitation-video-for-whatsapp--c1120)
- Vendor testimonials come from couples. Magical Star advises sending video to younger guests and close family, and static images to large lists (200–500+ guests) and to relatives with basic phones, because older relatives on low-storage phones struggle with 8–20 MB videos. — [Magical Star](https://magical-star.pages.dev/blog/video-invitation-vs-static-e-invite-india)
- Recommended formats: MP4 under 16 MB, and 30–45 second vertical reels for mobile. — [search summary of Happiffie and InviteCrafter pages](https://invitecrafter.com/blog/top-10-trending-video-invitation-styles-in-2026-for-weddings-&-events)
- A Hyderabad vendor says couples ask for multilingual toggles (English for NRI relatives in the US and UK, Telugu or Urdu for local guests). This is a single, unverified vendor claim. — [Happiffie Hyderabad](https://www.happiffie.com/hyderabad/connect-instantly-qr-smart-invitations)
- Gen-Z weddings are run as social media campaigns, with 7–15 second reels, drone shots, hashtags and wedding content creators. There are 4–9 pre-wedding events with 300–1,000 guests each. — [ThePrint, 26 Jan 2025](https://theprint.in/opinion/the-dating-story/the-reel-story-of-gen-z-weddings-how-they-are-lavish-viral-and-broke/2463119/)

### Inferences
- There are two buyers. Parents send the formal invite, often in Hindi or a regional script and in their own voice, to a large WhatsApp list, so a light file size matters. The couple buys save-the-dates and reels for Instagram and close friends.
- With 4–9 events per wedding, per-event invites and reminders are a natural multi-SKU product.

### Gaps
- Lead times for save-the-dates and invites (weeks before the wedding) in India. No sourced data.
- Usage shares for WhatsApp broadcast versus Status versus Instagram. Not found.
- No Reddit threads surfaced from r/IndianWeddings.

## Q4. Features buyers value

### Takeaway
Buyers value fast turnaround (24–48 hours), included revisions so names are spelled correctly, community and ritual-specific themes, illustrated or caricature couples, music, and WhatsApp-friendly file sizes. Regional-script support appears (Devanagari in templates, multilingual toggles), but its demand is not quantified.

### Cited Findings
- Turnaround of 24–48 hours with rush delivery available, and revisions included "so you do not pay extra to get your names spelled right". — [Magical Star](https://magical-star.pages.dev/blog/video-invitation-vs-static-e-invite-india)
- Community-specific themes (Sikh, South Indian, Bengali, Punjabi), Devanagari script in the "Flora Love" template, and illustrated or caricature couples. — [WedMeGood blog](https://www.wedmegood.com/blog/10-gorgeous-video-wedding-invites-that-cost-under-5k)
- Caricature, custom background and music are bundled in the standard tier. — [Fiverr mehak_jain](https://fiverr.com/mehak_jain/design-indian-wedding-invitation-video)
- WeddingWire links to "20+ Songs To Upgrade Your Wedding Invitation Video", which shows music matters. — [WeddingWire guide](https://www.weddingwire.in/wedding-tips/how-to-make-wedding-invitation-video-for-whatsapp--c1120)
- Multilingual NRI toggles (vendor claim). — [Happiffie Hyderabad](https://www.happiffie.com/hyderabad/connect-instantly-qr-smart-invitations)

### Inferences
- The product must let users get names, dates and venues correct themselves with free re-renders, offer an illustration or caricature option rather than only real photos, use licensed Indian music, deliver within minutes, and support multiple scripts.

### Gaps
- No quantitative preference data comparing real photos with caricatures, or showing language demand by state.

## Q5. Market size and trends

### Takeaway
CAIT estimates about 46 lakh weddings in the Nov–Dec 2025 season alone, worth ₹6.5 lakh crore, after about 48 lakh weddings and ₹5.9 lakh crore in the 2024 season. E-invite adoption is very high (about 91% used them in 2021-22), but physical cards persist (54% in 2024). I found no dedicated report sizing India's e-invite market.

### Cited Findings
- About 46 lakh weddings between 1 Nov and 14 Dec 2025, ₹6.5 lakh crore in trade (about $72B), from a CAIT Research report covering 75 cities. — [Angel One/CAIT](https://www.angelone.in/news/market-updates/indias-wedding-season-to-driveover-rs-6-lakh-crore-trade-says-cait), [Skift, 10 Dec 2025](https://skift.com/2025/12/10/indias-72-billion-wedding-season-is-reshaping-travel-again/). Skift's "460,000" figure appears to be a unit error.
- 2024 season: about 48 lakh weddings, ₹5.9 lakh crore. — [ThePrint/CAIT](https://theprint.in/economy/nov-dec-wedding-season-will-generate-business-of-rs-5-9-lakh-crore-cait/2290265)
- Kotak MF calls weddings India's fourth-largest industry, worth about $130B a year. — [Kotak MF blog](https://www.kotakmf.com/Information/blogs/indias-fourth-largest-industry-wedding-blitz_)
- India wedding services CAGR estimates: 14.3–15.3%. Digital invites are only a segment within these reports. — [Grand View Research](https://www.grandviewresearch.com/industry-analysis/india-wedding-services-market-report), [TechSci](https://techsciresearch.com/report/india-wedding-services-market/27872.html)
- Asia-Pacific wedding invitation printing: about $1.62B in 2025, growing 8.1% a year. This covers print, not digital. — [HTF MI](https://www.htfmarketintelligence.com/report/asia-pacific-wedding-invitation-printing-service-market)
- Digital communication grew during COVID in 2021, though no figures are given. — [WeddingWire guide](https://www.weddingwire.in/wedding-tips/how-to-make-wedding-invitation-video-for-whatsapp--c1120)

### Inferences
- About 10M weddings a year is plausible. Two seasonal windows of 4–5M each fit CAIT's figures, but I found no confirmed annual total. Illustrative only: if 30% of weddings bought one video invite at about ₹1,000, the spend would be roughly ₹300 crore a year. This is not a sourced number.
- Demand is extremely seasonal (Nov–Dec, plus Jan–Mar and Apr–Jun muhurat windows), so marketing and capacity must peak ahead of the season.

### Gaps
- No sourced annual wedding count, no e-invite spend figure, and no DIY versus done-for-you split.

## Q6. Why generic AI video tools fail to convert, and what converts

### Takeaway
I found no direct sourced post-mortems. Indirect evidence suggests conversion depends on cultural specificity (community and ritual themes), exact correctness of names and details, WhatsApp delivery constraints and hard deadlines, not on generic AI generation.

### Cited Findings
- Generic tools (VEED, Filmora, Simplified) market "Indian wedding invitation video makers", but specialised vendors compete on revisions, speed and community themes. — [VEED](https://www.veed.io/create/invitation-video-maker/indian-wedding-invitation-video-maker), [Filmora](https://filmora.wondershare.com/event-video/indian-wedding-invite-video.html), [Magical Star](https://magical-star.pages.dev/blog/video-invitation-vs-static-e-invite-india)
- Wedding content is driven by social display and family pride: lavish spending, reels, and parents spending about ₹90 lakh. — [ThePrint](https://theprint.in/opinion/the-dating-story/the-reel-story-of-gen-z-weddings-how-they-are-lavish-viral-and-broke/2463119/)

### Inferences
- Generic AI video likely fails because of wrong rituals or attire, garbled Indic text, uncanny faces of family members, and no guarantee that names and dates are exact. Templates with deterministic text layers and AI used only for styling (caricature from photo) are safer.

### Gaps
- No sourced case studies or reviews of AI invite tools failing. Reddit and Play Store reviews could not be reached.
